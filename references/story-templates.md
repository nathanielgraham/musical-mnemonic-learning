# Story Templates for Language Learning

Optional narrative layer for language lessons.  
Turn male/female dialogue into a simple continuing story so learners stay engaged while vocabulary is drilled.

## Currently Popular Romance / Romantasy Tropes (2025–2026)
Useful for beginner-friendly plots with high engagement:
- Fated mates / mate bond
- Billionaire + shifter (werewolf, dragon, etc.)
- Enemies to lovers
- Forced proximity
- Grumpy / sunshine
- Possessive / protective hero (“touch her and die”)
- Morally grey love interest
- Slow burn
- Found family (combined with romance)

## Design Rules
- 4–8 words per line.
- Male = one character (usually French), Female = the other (usually English reply).
- Single clear voice on all choruses.
- Chorus = pure mapping of the 4–6 newest vocabulary items.
- Recycle earlier vocabulary in verses.
- Plant one small unexplained hook per lesson for the next chapter.
- Keep each lesson ~2–3 minutes.

## 5-Lesson Outline Template
| Lesson | Plot beat                  | New vocabulary focus      |
|--------|----------------------------|---------------------------|
| 1      | Meeting + early reveal     | identity, status, yes/no  |
| 2      | Claim + resistance         | mine, free, stay, maybe   |
| 3      | Forced proximity           | here, night, cold, with me|
| 4      | Questions + protection     | why, because, secret, protect |
| 5      | Choice + resolution        | stay, leave, now, decide  |

## Full Example: Billionaire Werewolf (Beginner French)

**Style prompt used (adapted bubblegum):**  
```
90 BPM, major key, groovy 1960s psychedelic bubblegum pop rock. Male and female dialogue on question-answer pairs: deep chest-resonant baritone male voice, stoic measured cadence, commanding and authoritative with slight rasp, sings all French lines. Clear female voice replies with the English translations. Single clear female voice locked on every chorus. Female vocalist with light echo and delay. Bass run through fuzz pedal, massive pounding groove doubled and played as lead. Drums: metronome military cadence beat, massive pounding groove and fills. Lead guitar with effects pedal, massive feedback and wah-wah. Banjo played with slide and huge echo. Piano out of tune and played with overdrive. Trombone with mute and feedback. Mysterious yet inviting, slightly dangerous undertone, vocals forward and crisp, educational yet highly replayable, 1960s studio color.
```
**Note:** Voice separation and French chorus clarity remain inconsistent with this prompt. Single-voice regenerations are often clearer if intelligibility is the priority.
