# Voice Catalog

Reusable voice descriptions for Suno style prompts.  
Paste the relevant block into a style prompt when you want a specific vocal character.  
Keep descriptions short and high-signal — long character bios are often ignored.

## Clear Female (baseline)
Clear female vocalist, light echo and delay, crisp enunciation, forward in the mix, pleasant and intelligible.

## Stoic Commanding Male Baritone
Deep resonant baritone, chest-heavy, measured stoic cadence, commanding and authoritative, slight weathered rasp, grounded warmth, deliberate unhurried pacing, clean enunciation, timeless mid-Atlantic classical accent, clear enough for lyrics.

## Male/Female Dialogue (target language / English)
Male sings the target-language lines in a deep resonant baritone; clear female voice replies with the English. Single clear female voice locked on all choruses.

## Deep Warm Soul Baritone
Velvety bass-baritone, warm breathy timbre, smooth measured phrasing, subtle vibrato, powerful lower register, warm midrange, commanding yet tender.

## Cool Detached Post-Punk Female
Female vocalist, cool and restrained, slightly aloof but playful, deadpan and clear, rhythmic delivery, light Northern English accent, intelligible and forward.

## Pure Girl-Group Female
Pure girl-group female vocals, clear and bright, light 1950s-style backup harmonies, forward and crisp.

## Thin Reedy English Male (psychedelic)
Thin reedy high-pitched male vocalist with strong English accent, capable of deeper moments, sleepy lullaby phrasing, clear enough for lyrics.

## Quiet Gravelly Whisper (coiled authority)
Quiet gravelly whisper, deep chest-heavy register, tense coiled authority, breathy clenched articulation, slow unhurried pacing, calm deadpan murmur, forces the listener to lean in, clear enough for lyrics.
