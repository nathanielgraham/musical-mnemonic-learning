## Single-Voice Clean Groove (baseline)
**Use when:** Maximum clarity, consistent pronunciation, and earworm choruses. Best retention baseline.  
**Prompt:**
```
90 BPM, major key, groovy bubblegum pop, clear female vocals with light echo, strong simple drum groove, warm bassline you can tap to, bright clean electric guitar hooks, soft piano, catchy earworm chorus, rewarding melodic hooks, positive emotional peaks with satisfying resolutions, positive and cheerful, inviting and pleasant, low-to-moderate complexity, vocals forward and crisp, educational yet highly replayable
```
**Notes:** No voice split. Highest consistency for pure mapping/repetition.

## Psychedelic Bubblegum (Male/Female Q&A)
**Use when:** Beginner French with question-answer dialogue; male on French, female on English; single female voice locked on choruses.  
**Prompt:**
```
90 BPM, major key, groovy 1960s psychedelic bubblegum pop rock. Male and female dialogue on question-answer pairs: deep chest-resonant baritone male voice, stoic measured cadence, commanding and authoritative with slight rasp, sings all French lines. Clear female voice replies with the English translations. Single clear female voice locked on every chorus. Female vocalist with light echo and delay. Bass run through fuzz pedal, massive pounding groove doubled and played as lead. Drums: metronome military cadence beat, massive pounding groove and fills. Lead guitar with effects pedal, massive feedback and wah-wah. Banjo played with slide and huge echo. Piano out of tune and played with overdrive. Trombone with mute and feedback. Positive cheerful inviting mood, vocals forward and crisp, educational yet highly replayable, 1960s studio color.
```
**Notes:** Prioritizes voice split and chorus locking.


## Brutal Acid Techno / Japanese Shrine (learning-adapted)
**Use when:** Cold ritualistic atmosphere with enough structure for educational vocals.  
**Prompt:**
```
90 BPM, brutal acid techno fused with Japanese shrine ceremony, piercing nasal double reed with microtonal bends, bamboo mouth organ drone, high bamboo flute, dry lute and zither plucks, barrel drum and gong accents, clear female vocalist with restrained chant-like delivery, moderate hall reverb, steady pulse, vocals forward and intelligible, educational yet highly replayable
```
**Notes:** Free rhythm and unpitched shouts removed; a steady pulse and clear vocals added so the language can sit on top of the ritual atmosphere.
